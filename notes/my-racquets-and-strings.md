# My racquets & strings — personal notes

Last updated: 2026-10-12

## What I like in a string

- Reference setup I love: **Solinco Confidential 16L (1.25) @ 51 lbs in a Wilson Pro Staff 97 v14**,
  for its power, spin and control.
- That suggests I like a **firm, shaped co-poly that holds tension very well and grips the ball**.
  Confidential 16 (1.30) in TWU's lab at 51 lbs:
  - stiffness 222 lb/in
  - tension loss 22.2% (2nd-best of all polys tested)
  - string-to-ball friction 0.637
  - spin potential 6.4
  - TWU has no lab data for the 1.25 gauge.
- I want a controlled, predictable, lower launch, not a launchy or trampoline-y bed.

## Racquets

Published specs, not measured by me:

- Weight and balance: 332 g strung, 7 pts head-light.
- Swingweight 332.

- Beam 21.5 mm.
- Recommended tension 50–60 lbs.

### Wilson Defyer 98 Pro (current frame)
Published specs:
- Head and pattern: 98 sq in, **16x20**.
  - TW's playtester said the crosses feel spaced more like a 16x19.
- Weight: 305 g unstrung, about 323 g strung.
- Swingweight 319 (TW).
- Stiffness (TW flex rating) 64.
- Beam 22/23.5/22 mm.
- Recommended tension 47–57 lbs (TW "optimum 52").
- Reviewers' consensus:
  - low–moderate launch;
  - "won't blow you away with spin";
  - stock sweet spot on the small side;
  - responds very well to lead at 3 and 9.

## Current string job — Defyer 98 Pro

- **Toroline O‑Toro Tour 17 (1.23)**, full bed.
- **Mains 51 lbs / crosses 49 lbs.**
- Not pre-stretched.
- Strung 2026-10-12, before any lead.
- First hit (against the wall): **really nice**.

Why this string:
- It's the closest match to Confidential among the options I had. In TWU's lab at 51 lbs:
  - stiffness 217 lb/in
  - tension loss 25.0% (2nd-best of the candidates)
  - string-to-ball friction 0.608
- It adds spin, which the 16x20 Defyer needs: spin potential 7.7 vs 6.4.
- It's the lowest-launching of O‑Toro Tour / Razor Soft / Tour Sniper.
- Tennis Warehouse calls its trajectory "ultra-predictable".

Why 51/49:
- The crosses go 2 lbs lower because of the extra cross in 16x20. This lets the mains move and snap back.
- The mains stay at 51 because I plan to add lead.
  - Without lead, 50/48 was the suggestion, to make up for the lighter swingweight.

Things to watch:
- It takes about an hour to break in.
- A TW tester noticed the tension drop at about 10 hours.
  - **Log the hours.** If control drifts well before about 10 hours, ask for pre-stretch next time instead of raising tension.

## Lead plan

- Plan: lead at **3 and 9 o'clock**.
- Starting point: 2–3 g each side.
  - Rough estimate: about +2 swingweight per gram added in total, at 3 and 9.
  - From my measured 322, that would take it to about 330–334, which is the Pro Staff 97 v14's 332.
- **Re-measure after adding lead.**
- Lead at 3 and 9 mainly raises twistweight (stability and sweet spot) for only a modest swingweight gain.
  - Rackets & Runners added lead there on the Defyer and said it "worked wonders"; their swingweight went 317 → ~324.
- Tension guidance with lead:
  - keep 51/49;
  - drop back towards 50/48 if racquet-head speed suffers late in matches.

| 5 | Grapplesnake Tour Sniper full bed | 51 / 49 | 52 / 50 |

Notes on the shortlist:
- **#2** is untested anywhere; it's an idea from the data. Razor Soft has the slickest string-to-string surface among my options (0.070 friction).
- **Razor Soft:** Tecnifibre says not to string it below about 49 lbs. It launches higher, with more depth, than the other Tecnifibre polys, and it is "not a spin monster".
- **Wasabi / Caviar:** no lab data on either. Tension holding is reported as average (Wasabi) to mixed (Caviar).
- **Tour Sniper:** reviewers call it "ALU Power with more bite". Energy return is low (85.6%), and reports on how long it lasts are mixed.

Ruled out:
- **Razor Code:** too firm, 229 lb/in; low spin; TW rated its playability duration 69.
- **ALU Power / ALU Power Rough:** softer feel; fades after 8–10 h; the Rough loses tension badly unless pre-stretched.
- **Head Hawk Touch:** low spin and "too dead" in a control frame.
- **ReString Sync:** soft and stretchy; broke at about 8 h for one user.
- **ReString Slap:** no 1.25 gauge, only 1.23 or 1.28; "tinny"; launches higher; goes dead fast.

## TWU lab data used (51 lbs, fast swing)

Ranks are out of 100 among the newer-protocol polys.

| String | Stiffness (lb/in) | Tension loss | String/ball friction | Spin potential |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Confidential 16 (1.30) | 222 (84) | 22.2% (2) | 0.637 (94) | 6.4 |
| O‑Toro Tour 17 (1.23) | 217 (77) | 25.0% (8) | 0.608 (88) | 7.7 (87) |
| Razor Soft 17 (1.25) | 212 | 33.3% | 0.517 | 7.4 |
| Razor Code 17 (1.25) | 229 (90) | 29.6% | 0.480 | 5.6 |
| Tour Sniper 1.25 | 206 | 32.0% | 0.501 | 5.8 |
| ALU Power Rough 16L (1.25) | 209 | 39.6% (70) | 0.498 | 6.5 |
| Hawk Touch 18 (1.20) | 196 | 31.9% | 0.447 | 5.2 |

Launch-related numbers (O‑Toro Tour / Razor Soft / Tour Sniper):

| | O‑Toro Tour | Razor Soft | Tour Sniper |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tension left after stringing and settling | 38.3 lbs | 34.2 lbs | 34.6 lbs |
| Dwell time | 33.3 ms | 34.0 ms | 34.3 ms |
| Stringbed deflection | 31.0 mm | 31.7 mm | 31.9 mm |

O‑Toro Tour plays tightest, so it gives the lowest launch.

Caveats about the data:
- **Two test protocols.** The CSV (`string_performance_database.csv`) mixes two TWU test protocols. The older rows show much higher tension loss (median 46.5% vs 33.3%), so only compare tension loss within the same protocol.
  - ALU Power 125 is an older-protocol row.
- **Gauge and stretch columns are unreliable.** TWU's "Gauge Actual" and "Stretch" values change depending on how the site is queried.
- **No lab data in TWU** for ReString Sync or Slap, Toroline Caviar or Wasabi, or Confidential 16L (1.25).

## General principles I've picked up

- **16x20 vs 16x19:** a denser bed plays firmer and lower at the same tension. Drop the crosses about 2 lbs to let the mains move.
- **Swingweight:** lower swingweight means less plow and depth.
  - You can drop tension 1–2 lbs to compensate.
  - If you add mass instead, keep the tension.
- **Tension vs string choice:** tension changes launch more than the difference between similar co-polys does.
- **Toroline strings:** Tennisnerd says they're usually strung 2–3 lbs lower than a traditional stiff poly like Confidential.
- **Pre-stretching:** it reduces the later rate of tension loss (Cross & Bower, *Sports Engineering*, 2001).
  - It matters most for strings that drop tension fast.
  - It's optional for good tension-holders like O‑Toro Tour.
